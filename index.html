Play Dominoes — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Play Dominoes ("the app," "we," "us"), formerly named Dominoes Arena, is a competitive multiplayer dominoes game for Android. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it. It applies to the app and the services it talks to.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhatWhy
Account Email address, display name, encrypted password (we never see your plaintext password), account creation date. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and name from Google — never your contacts or photos. To create and secure your account, and to identify you to other players by display name.
Gameplay & competitive data Match history and results, ELO rating, Grand Prix ranking points, reputation level, tournament registrations and results, in-app coin balance and transaction history. To run matches and tournaments, calculate rankings, and show you your own history and stats.
Social features Your friends list, friend requests, and direct messages you send to friends inside the app. To provide the friends and messaging features you choose to use.
Profile photo (optional) If you choose to upload one, your avatar image. Visible to other players, like a public profile picture. To personalize your profile.
Optional profile details Your country (optional; shown to other players as a flag), and your age group and gender (optional; you may answer "prefer not to say"). Age group and gender are never shown to other players. You can change or clear all three at any time in Account settings, and they are deleted with your account. Country: to show your flag. Age group and gender: only to produce anonymous, aggregate statistics that help us understand who plays and improve the game. We do not use them to single out an individual.
Push notification data A device push token, your device's platform (Android/iOS), and app version — only if you grant notification permission. Also your notification category preferences (reminders, tournament promotions, offers, social). To deliver the specific notifications you've opted into, and respect the ones you've turned off.
Crash & diagnostic data Automatically collected on a crash: device model, OS version, app version, and the error/stack trace. Handled by Firebase Crashlytics. To find and fix bugs.
Usage analytics Aggregate, non-identifying app usage events (e.g. which screens/features are used), handled by Firebase Analytics. To understand which parts of the app are working and which need improvement.
Support reports If you submit a bug report, problem report, or idea from inside the app, its text content plus your account id, the app version, your device platform and language, the screen you were on, and the match or tournament it refers to. A copy (with a shortened account id, no email) is also sent to a private Telegram chat that only we can read. So we can read and act on it.
Chat messages Messages you type in the lobby chat (shown to everyone in the lobby; the server keeps only the most recent lines in memory, and they are lost when it restarts) and in a table's chat (shown to the players at that table, and kept with the match while it is in play), with your display name. Friend direct messages are described above. To let players talk to each other. Do not share personal information in chat.
Advertising data When ads are shown, our ad mediation partner and the ad networks it works with receive your device's advertising ID, device and network information (such as device model, OS version, language, and IP address), and ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, completed, or tapped). To show ads (including optional ads you choose to watch for in-game coins), confirm that a rewarded ad was completed, measure ad performance, and prevent ad fraud.
Purchase data If you buy something in the app (coin packs, ad-free time, the Starter Bundle, or a Competitor or Arena subscription): the product, the Google Play order ID and purchase token, the purchase time, the country reported by Google Play, whether it was a test purchase, the subscription period and status, and any refund. We never see or store your card or other payment details; Google Play handles the payment. To deliver what you bought (coins, ad-free time, pass benefits), keep your passes up to date, undo a purchase if Google refunds it, prevent fraud, and keep financial records.

Ads

Play Dominoes may show ads, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource and Unity Ads) and the ad networks it mediates, including Google AdMob.

  • Personalized ads are off by default. You can turn them on or off at any time with the "Personalized ads" switch on the app's Account screen. When off, ads may still appear but are not personalized using your device data.
  • You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • In-game coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

In-app purchases

Play Dominoes sells optional in-app purchases for real money through Google Play: coin packs, timed ad-free passes, a Starter Bundle, and the Competitor Pass and Arena Pass subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; refunds and subscription cancellations are managed in Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

  • Coins are an in-game currency for table and tournament access. They have no cash value and cannot be cashed out. There are no real-money prizes.
  • Rankings, ELO and Grand Prix points are earned by playing and can never be bought.
  • Passes remove forced ads and change what you can access. They never change your rating or points.
  • If Google Play refunds a purchase, we remove the coins or benefits it gave. If you have already spent the coins, a balance may be recorded against your account.
  • When you delete your account, we keep the financial record of each purchase (product, order ID, time, country and amount of coins or time delivered) for accounting, fraud prevention and legal reasons, with your account identifier removed.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

3. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers to run the app. Each only receives the data needed to do its job. Apart from the advertising partners listed below, none of them may use your data for their own advertising:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and your uploaded profile photo.
  • Google Play (Google Play Billing) — processes purchases and subscriptions. We receive the order and purchase details listed above, not your payment details.
  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Crashlytics (crash reports), and Analytics (aggregate usage).
  • Our game server host (Railway) — runs the real-time match server your device connects to during a game.
  • Telegram — receives a copy of the support reports you send from the app, in a private chat only we can read (see "Support reports" above).
  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource, Unity Ads) and the ad networks it mediates — deliver and measure ads as described in the Ads section. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies, for example Unity's player privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — one of the ad networks Unity LevelPlay mediates; it delivers and measures ads using your device's advertising ID and general device information. See how Google uses data in advertising.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our players or the app.

4. Data retention and deleting your Play Dominoes account

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so your rating, history, and friends list stay intact.

In the app: open your Account screen and tap Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately. (If you are seated at a table, have coins held in a match, or are registered in a live tournament, finish or withdraw first.)

By email, if you can't use the app:

  1. Email gblancocpa@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Play Dominoes account".
  2. Include your display name so we can find the right account.
  3. We confirm the request by replying to that email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted: your account (email, display name, sign-in credentials), profile photo, friends list and direct messages, match history, ratings and rankings, coin balance and transaction history, push notification tokens and preferences, and support reports.

What may be kept: limited records we are required to keep for fraud prevention, security, or legal reasons (for example, logs of reported abuse), for up to 90 days unless the law requires longer. Match results already shown to other players may remain in their history in anonymized form, without your name. A copy of a support report in our private Telegram chat is not linked to your name or email (only a shortened account id); ask us by email and we will remove it. Crash and analytics data held by Firebase is not linked to your name and expires on Firebase's own retention schedule.

Play Dominoes is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using commercially reasonable means.
