Dominoes Arena — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Dominoes Arena ("the app," "we," "us") is a competitive multiplayer dominoes game for Android. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control or delete it. It applies to the app and the services it talks to.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhatWhy
Account Email address, display name, encrypted password (we never see your plaintext password), account creation date. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and name from Google — never your contacts or photos. To create and secure your account, and to identify you to other players by display name.
Gameplay & competitive data Match history and results, ELO rating, Grand Prix ranking points, reputation level, tournament registrations and results, in-app coin balance and transaction history. To run matches and tournaments, calculate rankings, and show you your own history and stats.
Social features Your friends list, friend requests, and direct messages you send to friends inside the app. To provide the friends and messaging features you choose to use.
Profile photo (optional) If you choose to upload one, your avatar image. Visible to other players, like a public profile picture. To personalize your profile.
Push notification data A device push token, your device's platform (Android/iOS), and app version — only if you grant notification permission. Also your notification category preferences (reminders, tournament promotions, offers, social). To deliver the specific notifications you've opted into, and respect the ones you've turned off.
Crash & diagnostic data Automatically collected on a crash: device model, OS version, app version, and the error/stack trace. Handled by Firebase Crashlytics. To find and fix bugs.
Usage analytics Aggregate, non-identifying app usage events (e.g. which screens/features are used), handled by Firebase Analytics. To understand which parts of the app are working and which need improvement.
Support reports If you submit a bug report, problem report, or idea from inside the app, its text content plus your account id. So we can read and act on it.

We do not currently show ads or sell any in-app purchases — if that changes in the future, this policy will be updated first, and that section will say so explicitly.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.
  • We do not read or collect data from other apps on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

3. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers to run the app. Each only receives the data needed to do its job, and none of them may use it for their own advertising:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and your uploaded profile photo.
  • Google Firebase — Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Crashlytics (crash reports), and Analytics (aggregate usage).
  • Our game server host (Railway/Fly.io) — runs the real-time match server your device connects to during a game.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our players or the app.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active, so your rating, history, and friends list stay intact. If you want your account and personal data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will delete it, except where we're required to keep certain records (for example, fraud-prevention logs) for a limited time as permitted by law.

5. Children's privacy

Dominoes Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS) between the app and our servers, and database-level access controls (row-level security) that restrict who can read or write your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using commercially reasonable means.

7. International data

Our service providers may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
